# Review A: Temporal Sampling Frame Coherence

Scope: the 19-row yearly plan + 1105-row monthly ledger generated by
`scripts/build_temporal_discovery_ledger.py` from
`sampling/frame.yaml::temporal_tiers`. This review attacks the
per-year denominator structure only.

## Q1. Three-tier boundary defensibility

The boundary definitions are at `sampling/frame.yaml:27-49` and the
script's tier selector at
`scripts/build_temporal_discovery_ledger.py:137-151`.

- 2008 = Bitcoin genesis era; defensible as discovery-ledger floor
  (`sampling/frame.yaml:13`).
- **2013 cut is weak.** The tier prose (`frame.yaml:36-43`) does not
  name what 2012 lacks. Empirically the cut tracks the first
  US-federal candidates (`sec-shavers-btcst-2013` 2013-07,
  `silk-road-doj-seizure-2013` 2013-10) — defensible but should be
  documented as "first admission-grade public enforcement" rather
  than a calendar slot.
- **2017 cut is principled.** It coincides with Censored Planet L0
  onset (`docs/paper_claims.md:286` declares `not_available_pre_2018`
  for L0) and the OFAC-recent-actions extractor's first
  crypto-relevant content (the 2015-2016 entries in
  `ofac-recent-actions-triage.json` are explicitly
  `no_crypto_content`). L0 substrate, OFAC's first crypto SDN
  (2018-11), and relayed-block-level instrumentation all cluster
  around 2017-2018.

"comparable_main" under-specifies. The prose ("main corpus for
comparable cross-layer observability claims") should instead say
*measurement-substrate coverage for L0 (Censored Planet 2018+) and
L1 (PBS post-Merge 2022-09+) exists at non-degenerate levels, so
coverage-matched conditional rates per layer are defined for events
in this window*. Otherwise the boundary reads as a calendar choice.

Verdict: 2017 boundary defensible; 2013 boundary needs an explicit
substrate-availability rationale.

## Q2. Why are `_2017_2026` frames applied to 2008-2016?

This is the most serious frame defect. Every source frame's
`source_frame_id` is slugged `_2017_2026` (`sampling/frame.yaml:85,
101, 117, 133, 150`) yet each frame's `date_range` is set to
`2008-01-01 → 2026-12-31` (`sampling/frame.yaml:87, 103, 119, 135,
152`) and each carries `tiered_date_ranges` covering
`discovery_only_2008_2012` (`sampling/frame.yaml:90, 105, 121, 137,
154`). The ledger script then materializes 5 frames × 221 months =
1105 rows including 300 rows in 2008-2012 for frames that cannot
return a candidate there.

Concrete evidence the 300 pending 2008-2012 rows are
**structurally-misframed, not honestly-pending**:

- `ofac_recent_actions_crypto_2017_2026` extractor manifest first
  entry is 2015-04 (`ofac-recent-actions-triage.json:2-9`); 2008-2014
  is not searchable in the declared source artifact.
- `us_federal_enforcement_crypto_2017_2026` triage manifest is 4
  total rows (`sources/source_frame_triage/us_federal_enforcement_archives.csv`,
  `wc -l` = 4), with the earliest at 2013-03.
- The other three triage files (corporate_policy, eu_un_fatf,
  non_us_regulators_fius) have only a header (`wc -l` = 1) —
  manifests are stubs.

So of the 300 `pending` 2008-2012 monthly cells, all 300 are
"frame-extends-but-source-artifact-does-not." The honest status for
the OFAC frame's 2008-2014 cells is `source_unavailable` or
`not_applicable_pre_market`; for non-OFAC frames with empty triage
manifests it is `pending` only after a triage manifest exists.
`not_applicable_pre_market` is declared in
`scripts/build_temporal_discovery_ledger.py:50` and counted at
line 507 but **never assigned anywhere** in the script — it is dead
code. Pending is doing the work of three distinct statuses.

## Q3. Denominator coherence

`source_frame_months = 60` is wrong for a yearly admission claim:
(1) it fuses 5 *heterogeneous* frames (supranational has 2 in-frame
units total, `frame.yaml:163`; OFAC has 26) — month-multiplication
washes that out; (2) a yearly admission *rate* wants
*admitted_events / distinct_in_frame_triggers*, which the plan
already carries as column 11. `source_frame_months` is a
collection-coverage signal, not a measurement denominator.
`yearly_collection_plan.md:5` rightly disclaims paper-denominator
use — keep it that way; do not let `source_frame_months` leak into
any rate.

## Q4. Cross-tier admission distribution (0 / 0 / 52)

- 0 admitted in 2008-2012 is correct by design
  (`yearly_collection_plan.md:32` "Historical-baseline rows can become
  full YAML events, but stay out of 2017+ comparable denominators").
- 0 admitted in 2013-2016 is **not** "deferred admission" — it is
  *zero promoted events* despite 12 candidate-found months and 14
  candidate stubs (rows 7-10 of `yearly_collection_plan.csv`,
  columns 8-9; see also `ls events/*201[3-6]*` → no match). The
  historical_baseline tier is therefore window dressing at v0.2: it
  is a *staging area for candidates that have not been promoted*. If
  the v0.2 release ships with zero historical_baseline event YAMLs,
  the tier collapses to a discovery ledger plus a "rejected because
  pre-2017" filter; it does not earn its own analytical name.
- 52 admitted in 2017-present matches `events/*.yaml` count (count
  by year filename: 2+2+1+3+5+14+16+6+8 = 57 files, of which 52
  admitted per `docs/paper_claims.md:25`; 5 are rejected /
  null / candidate forms).

## Q5. Per-year admission inside comparable_main

1/2017, 2/2018, 1/2019, 3/2020, 5/2021, 13/2022, 14/2023, 5/2024,
8/2025, 0/2026 (`yearly_collection_plan.csv:11-20`).

The 2022-2023 surge is plausibly real: 2022-08 Tornado SDN, 2022-09
Merge, post-FTX enforcement wave. Candidate-found counts by source
frame show the spike is driven by *all five* frames simultaneously
admitting more rows (corporate=2,non_us=2,ofac=6,supra=2,fed=4 in
2022; similar in 2023), consistent with a real enforcement shift,
not a single-frame artifact.

2024 (5 admitted) is suspicious. 2025 has 9 ofac candidate-found
months but only 8 admitted — under-promotion relative to candidates;
2024 has 48 pending vs only 12 candidate-found months. Without a
closed 2024 triage manifest, the year cannot be defended as "low
enforcement" — only as "low triage completion."

## Q6. Pending (1000/1105 = 90.5%) — TODO or sampling-frame defect?

Both — pending is being asked to do two jobs at once. The two jobs
need to separate:

- *Honest TODO* — cells where a triage manifest could plausibly find
  a candidate (e.g., 2024 non-US state frame months not yet
  searched). This is backlog, not a frame defect.
- *Structural absence* — cells where the source artifact does not
  cover the window (OFAC recent-actions 2008-2014) or the frame
  unit cannot exist (corporate_policy pre-2014, when on-chain
  issuer/exchange operators that this frame targets did not
  meaningfully exist). These should be `source_unavailable` or
  `not_applicable_pre_market`, not `pending`.

Pending becomes a release blocker when any paper claim cites a
frame-level admission rate. `docs/paper_claims.md:201-249` does not;
the frame is invoked descriptively ("US-trigger-dominant"). Pending
is *not currently* a v0.2 release blocker, but it is a blocker for
any v0.3+ claim of form "X% of in-frame triggers were admitted."

## Severity-ranked defects

### Fatal (1)

**F1. `source_frame_id` slug claims 2017-2026 while
`date_range` and `tiered_date_ranges` declare 2008-2026** — the slug
mis-advertises the frame's temporal coverage, and the script
materializes 300 monthly cells that are structurally unreachable by
the declared `source_artifact` (OFAC recent-actions extractor begins
2015-04; 4 of 5 triage manifests are header-only stubs).
`sampling/frame.yaml:85-164`,
`scripts/build_temporal_discovery_ledger.py:173-187`.

*Minimal fix:* Either (a) rename slugs to `_2008_2026` to match the
declared date_range, or (b) shrink each frame's `date_range` to the
earliest date its `source_artifact` can return — and in the latter
case, the discovery_only_2008_2012 tier should be dropped or
documented as out-of-frame. Choice (a) keeps the ledger; choice (b)
removes 300 phantom-pending rows. Recommend (a) + introducing
`source_artifact_first_available_date` per frame so the ledger can
emit `source_unavailable` for pre-artifact cells.

### Major (1)

**M1. `not_applicable_pre_market` and `source_unavailable` are
declared but never assigned** — `pending` is doing the work of three
distinct statuses, and the cross-tier 0/0/52 admission split looks
deliberate but actually reflects unfinished triage masquerading as
"in-frame but unsearched."
`scripts/build_temporal_discovery_ledger.py:50, 507`.

*Minimal fix:* In `tier_for_month` / `empty_row`, set
`ledger_status = source_unavailable` when the discovery_month is
strictly before a frame's `source_artifact_first_available_date`
(new field). Set `not_applicable_pre_market` for pre-2014 corporate
and pre-2017 supranational cells where the frame's stratum target
(e.g., `S5_corporate` issuer/exchange operators) did not exist as an
observable population. This removes the misleading 300 pending count
without adding new admission rules.

### Minor (1)

**m1. Historical_baseline tier (2013-2016) has 0 admitted events
across 4 years and 14 candidate stubs** — the tier name implies a
denominator role but currently functions as a candidate parking lot.
`yearly_collection_plan.csv:7-10`, `events/*201[3-6]*` empty.

*Minimal fix:* Either promote at least 2 of the 14 candidate stubs
to admitted event YAMLs before v0.2 release (the silk-road and
sec-shavers candidates are anchored cases in the field literature
and should clear admission), or rename the tier
`historical_candidate_pool_2013_2016` and stop framing it as a
denominator-eligible window. Promoting 2 anchor cases is the lower-
scope option and earns the tier its name.

## Verdict

**Yes-with-conditions.** The 2017 boundary is principled, the
admission-rate distribution within 2017-present is defensible
against a "real enforcement shift" reading, and the v0.2 paper
claims do not yet cite the temporal ledger as a denominator. But F1
and M1 together produce a ledger where 27% of monthly cells (300 of
1105) are structurally unreachable yet appear in `pending`,
inflating the "uncompleted triage" bar and obscuring the boundary
between *we haven't searched* and *the source frame doesn't extend
back that far*. Address F1 (rename or shrink date ranges + new
`source_artifact_first_available_date`) and M1 (assign the unused
statuses) before v0.2 release; m1 can defer to v0.3.
