Archive of Changes to OFAC's Sanctions Lists

These archive files show all of the changes that have been made to OFAC's sanctions lists for the life of each list, except in the case of the list of Specially Designated Nationals where records are maintained here back to 1994. OFAC does not maintain "historic" or "archived" versions of its actual lists. The office only maintains active versions of its sanctions lists for policy and legal reasons. Members of the public that are interested in older entries on OFAC's sanctions lists can use this archive to look up specific entries by year. Complete information on when each name was added, modified, or removed from a list are stored in each file.

SDN List archives are available from 2001 to present in PDF format here and are available from 1994 to 2023 in text format farther down this page.
