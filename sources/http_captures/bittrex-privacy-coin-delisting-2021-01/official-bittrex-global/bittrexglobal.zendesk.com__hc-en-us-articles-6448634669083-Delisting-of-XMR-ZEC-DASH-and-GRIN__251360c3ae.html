Delisting of XMR, ZEC, DASH, and GRIN

Updated August 31, 2022 22:11

Bittrex Global will be removing Monero, Dash, Zcash, and Grin coins from Bittrex Global.

The following markets will be removed for trading on January 15, 2021, 23:00 UTC:

● Dash: BTC-DASH, ETH-DASH, USDT-DASH, USD-DASH
● Grin: BTC-GRIN *, USDT-GRIN *
● Monero: BTC-XMR, ETH-XMR, USDT-XMR
● Zcash: BTC-ZEC, ETH-ZEC, USDT-ZEC, USD-ZEC

* May 14, 2021: Markets will be removed on Friday, May 21, 2021 at 5:00 PM UTC

There may be circumstances under which a user may not be able to withdraw a coin or token
due to events outside of Bittrex Global’s control. For example, technical difficulties (e.g., the
blockchain is broken) may prevent users from being able to withdraw their coins or tokens.

If you have any questions about your Monero, Zcash, Dash, or Grin holdings, please open a
support ticket and our Customer Service team will respond as soon as possible.
