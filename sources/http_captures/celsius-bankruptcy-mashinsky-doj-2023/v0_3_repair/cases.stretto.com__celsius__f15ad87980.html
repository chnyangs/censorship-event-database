    On July 13, 2022, Celsius Network LLC and certain of its affiliates (collectively, the “Celsius Debtors”) filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York.  The cases of the Debtors are pending before the Honorable Martin Glenn in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York and are being jointly administered under Case No. 22-10964.

    On November 9, 2023, the Bankruptcy Court entered the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Order Confirming the Modified Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Celsius Network LLC and Its Debtor Affiliates (the “Confirmation Order”), pursuant to which the Bankruptcy Court approved and confirmed the Modified Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of Celsius Network LLC and Its Debtor Affiliates (the “Plan”).

     

    Notice Regarding Completed Distribution Communications and Ongoing Phishing Attempts

    On April 25, 2025, the Post-Effective Date Debtors began sending emails, as further detailed in the Notice Regarding Completed Distribution Communications and Ongoing Phishing Attempts to creditors who have Claims in Class 4 (Convenience Claims), Class 6A (General Custody Claims), or Class 6B (Withdrawable Custody Claims) who have fully redeemed their distributions under the Plan.

    The Post-Effective Date Debtors are aware of continued phishing attempts purporting to be related to these Chapter 11 Cases. For example, creditors have reported emails  purportedly from Celsius or Stretto asking them to log into a “claims portal” to provide their wallet information and receive their distribution. Many of these emails state that the distribution will be made in USDC or ether. These emails also include a one-time passcode or authorization code to access the “claims portal” or support ticket system.

    For the avoidance of doubt, the second distribution will only be made in bitcoin or Cash.

    Celsius is not making distributions directly to creditors’ wallets and also does not send a one-time passcode or authorization code as part of an email informing a creditor of their distribution status. Rather, the one-time passcode used to log in to the legitimate Celsius Claims Portal is sent when a creditor takes the affirmative action to log in to such portal.

    Please review the instructions set forth in Effective Date Notice above, on the Debtors’ Celsius X account @CelsiusNetwork, and on Stretto's Celsius Distributions Knowledge Base (the “Knowledge Base”) for further information and continuing updates. The Knowledge Base will continually be updated with the latest information.

    Notice of Phishing Attempts

    PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that neither the Debtors nor their advisors will ever contact you by email, telephone call, or otherwise requesting account information or other personal information absent an Order from the Court.

     

    If you receive any message purporting to be from the Debtors or their advisors and requesting account information or personal information, contact the Debtors immediately at CelsiusCreditorQuestions@kirkland.com or the Debtors’ claims agent, Stretto, at CelsiusInquiries@stretto.com.

     

    To report spoofing or phishing attempts, or to report that you've been a victim, file a complaint with the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).
