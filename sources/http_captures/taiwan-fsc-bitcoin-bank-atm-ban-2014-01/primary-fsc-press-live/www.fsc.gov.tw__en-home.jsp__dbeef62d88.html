Financial Institutions' ATMs may not Provide Related Services for Bitcoin

2014-01-06
 
    The FSC provides the following explanation in response to recent media reports on the proposed entry of Bitcoin ATMs in Hong Kong and Taiwan in January 2014 as a result of strong demand for Bitcoin in Asia:
　
     An automated teller machine (ATM) refers to an automated service facility established by a bank outside a place its business for cash withdrawal and other financial transaction services based on an application for the approval of the FSC filed in accordance with Article 57, Paragraph 2 of the Banking Act and the "Regulations Governing Off-Premises Automatic Teller Machines of Financial Institutions". Bitcoin is not a currency but a "virtual asset". It cannot be used as a payment tools commonly accepted by the general public. Therefore, banks and other financial institutions may not accept or exchange Bitcoin or provide related services for Bitcoin through bank ATMs.
　
　The FSC states that if citizens accept, trade, or hold Bitcoin, they should have correct knowledge of its non-currency nature to prevent transaction risks and consumer disputes.
