In light of recent developments by the French regulator, and in continuation of the restrictions we previously implemented in the country, Bybit will no longer provide withdrawal and custody services to nationals or residents of the French Territories ("French Users") starting from Jan 8, 2025.

 

After Jan 8, users with assets valued at 10 USDC or less will incur a 10 USDC fee and have their accounts closed. For users with assets exceeding 10 USDC, we will initiate the transfer of any remaining funds to our trusted custody partner, Coinhouse. Subsequent withdrawals will need to be processed through their platform.

 

This guide helps Bybit French users register for a Coinhouse account, and complete identity verification to have their assets transferred to Coinhouse seamlessly.

Coinhouse Onboard Steps

Step 1: Visit https://www.coinhouse.com/ and click Get Started or download the Coinhouse App.

 




Step 2: Enter your Email Address and click on Create Account.

 




Step 3: Check your email inbox for a verification link. Click the link to confirm your email.

 

 




Step 4: Set a secured password and click Continue.

 




Step 5: Download or open the Coinhouse App and log in using your email and password.

 



Step 6: Create a security code to secure your app and choose Personal profile type.

 




Step 7: Fill in your details accordingly and verify your phone number.

 




Step 8: Agree to the terms and conditions to begin the identity verification. 

 

Important: You are highly encouraged to use the same identity document linked to your Bybit account to complete the verification for seamless asset transfers.

 




Step 9: Follow these steps to verify your identity:

  • Scan your ID by positioning it within the camera frame.

  • Tilt the ID up and down to display the hologram.

  • Take a selfie video when prompted.

 




Step 10: Enter your phone number for verification code. 

 




Step 14: Review and digitally sign your documents by entering the verification code sent to your phone.

 

 

Congratulations! You have successfully completed the verification. 

 



If the identity on your Coinhouse account matches the identity on your Bybit account, your assets on Bybit will be auto-credited to your Coinhouse account within 2 working days.






Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this change happening?

In light of recent developments by the French regulator and as part of our commitment to comply with all relevant regulations, we regret to inform you that Bybit will no longer provide withdrawal and custody services to users in the French Territories starting Jan 8, 2025.



When will Bybit cease offering services to French users?

Bybit will cease withdrawal and asset custody services for French users on Jan 8, 2025, at 8:00 UTC. French users who do not withdraw their crypto and fiat assets by this date will be subject to one of the two procedures outlined in the next FAQ.




What will happen to my assets and Bybit account after Jan 8, 2025, 8AM UTC?

  • For Users with Assets Valued at 10 USDC or Less: A 10 USDC termination fee will be charged against your available crypto and/or fiat assets at the prevailing conversion rate. After the fee is deducted, your account will be permanently closed.

  • For Users with Assets Above 10 USDC: Your fiat and crypto assets will be withdrawn from your Bybit accounts. These assets will then be converted into USDC at the prevailing conversion rate, after which the transfer to our trusted custody partner, Coinhouse will begin. As a result, custody fees will commence until you complete the identity verification on Coinhouse. 

We strongly advise you to make a withdrawal before this date.




Who is Coinhouse?

Coinhouse, a regulated French crypto assets platform, is a trusted third-party platform that provides secure custody, trading, and withdrawal services for digital assets, offering tailored investment advice and support to individuals and businesses.




Can I withdraw my assets from Bybit before Jan 8, 2025?

Yes, you are strongly encouraged to make a withdrawal from your Bybit Account before Jan 8, 2025.




What are the key dates to remember?

  • Jan 8, 2025, at 8:00 UTC: 

Bybit withdrawal and custody services for French users end. Users with assets valued at 10 USDC or lesser will have their Bybit account permanently closed. 

  • Jan 8 at 8AM UTC to Jan 16 at 8AM UTC:  

For users with assets valued above 10 USDC, all of your fiat and crypto assets on Bybit will be converted into USDC, and transfers to Coinhouse for custody will take place. Withdrawals will be unavailable from Coinhouse until the transfer is completed. 

  • Jan 16, 2025, at 8:00 UTC: 

Assets will be transferred to Coinhouse. If you already have a Coinhouse account and the identity matches the details on your Bybit account, your assets will be credited to your Coinhouse account automatically within 2 working days. 

If you do not have a Coinhouse account, you will need to open one and complete the identity verification process. Once verified, your assets will be available for withdrawal from Coinhouse. 




What if I do not have enough balance to cover the 10 USDC termination fee?
The fee will be deducted from your available crypto or fiat assets at the prevailing conversion rate. If the balance is insufficient, the account closure will still proceed.




How can I create a Coinhouse Account and complete the identity verification?

You can register here by following this guide. Ensure you use the same identity details as on your Bybit account to complete the identity verification on Coinhouse.




Do I need to use the same identity document I used on Bybit to complete Coinhouse verification?

It is acceptable to use a different document as long as it belongs to the same identity registered on Bybit. However, using the same document is recommended to ensure a seamless transfer of your assets to your Coinhouse account.




What if I fail to create or verify my Coinhouse account?

You can reach out to Coinhouse’s Customer Support by submitting a request here. Their team will respond to your inquiry within 24 hours.




What is the Custody Fee?

A monthly custody and no activity fee of 0.16% of the total value of your assets or 1 USDC equivalent (whichever is higher) will be charged. This fee will only apply while your assets remain with Coinhouse but you have not completed the KYC verification. Once you verify your Coinhouse account to match your Bybit identity, the monthly fee will no longer apply. 




When will Custody fees start to apply?

The first fee will be charged on the day the assets are transferred to Coinhouse. After that, it will be charged on the 1st of each month until you create the Coinhouse account and complete identity verification. Users with a verified account matching their Bybit identity will not incur this fee and will see their assets reflected in their account shortly after the transfer.




Will both my fiat and crypto assets be transferred to Coinhouse?

Yes, both fiat and crypto assets will be converted into USDC and transferred to Coinhouse for custody. 




Will I be charged for the conversion to USDC or the transfer to Coinhouse?

No, there are no fees for the conversion to USDC or the transfer to Coinhouse. However, all remaining crypto and/or fiat assets on Bybit will be converted to USDC at the prevailing conversion rate on Jan 8, 2025, at 8:00 UTC. 




I have created and verified my Coinhouse account, when will I receive my assets?

You will receive your assets within 2 working days if your identity matches the one on your Bybit account.




I had multiple types of tokens on my Bybit account. Why do I only see USDC on my Coinhouse account?

All your assets on Bybit have been converted into USDC before being transferred to Coinhouse. You will receive a statement detailing your final asset holdings on Bybit, the conversion rates applied, and the total USDC transferred to your Coinhouse account. If you notice any discrepancies, they may be due to the monthly custody fees charged.
