HTX to Gradually Retire Existing Mainland China Users

2021/09/26 07:30
Latest News
Others
由
Translate
Translate

Dear Valued Users,
 

To comply with local laws and regulations, HTX has ceased account registration for new users in Mainland China, effective September 24, 2021 (UTC+8).
 

HTX will gradually retire existing Mainland China user accounts by 24:00 (UTC+8) on December 31, 2021, to ensure the safety of users' assets. We will inform users of the specific arrangements and details through official announcements, e-mails, inbox messages, etc. The details on cessation of account registration will not be sent by text messages, and the final explanation is subject to the official announcement. We also kindly remind you to stay alert to scams.
 

HTX has always been dedicated to offering digital asset trading services and ensuring the safety of customer assets while following all applicable laws. We apologize for any inconvenience caused and thank you for your understanding and support!
