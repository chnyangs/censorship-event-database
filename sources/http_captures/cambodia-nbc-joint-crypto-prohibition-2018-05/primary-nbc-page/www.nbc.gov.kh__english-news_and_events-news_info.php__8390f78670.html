Joint Statement between The national Bank of Cambodia, the Securities and Exchange Commission of Cambodia and the General-Commissariat of National Police
2018-07-13

The National Bank of Cambodia appeals that the public be cautious of the propagation to mobilize funds and the operations of buying, selling, trading and settlement of Crypto Currencies without obtaining license from competent authorities.
Any person or legal entity that propagates to mobilize funds, buys, sells, trades or settles Crypto Currencies without obtaining license from competent authorities shall be penalized in accordance with applicable laws.
The National Bank of Cambodia believes that the public shall strictly implement the spirit of this statement.
