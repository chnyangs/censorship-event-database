Frequently Asked Questions

Is this address blocked on USDT?
Yes, this Ethereum address is currently on the USDT blacklist managed by Tether. The address was blocked with a balance of 19,694.80 USDT. Transactions involving this address will be rejected by the USDT smart contract.
Can funds be withdrawn from a blacklisted USDT address?
No. When an address is added to the USDT blacklist by Tether, all USDT transfers from and to this address are blocked at the smart contract level. The funds are effectively frozen and cannot be moved.
