Organization: Archive Team
Formed in 2009, the Archive Team (not to be confused with the archive.org Archive-It Team) is a rogue archivist collective dedicated to saving copies of rapidly dying or deleted websites for the sake of history and digital heritage. The group is 100% composed of volunteers and interested parties, and has expanded into a large amount of related projects for saving online and digital history.

History is littered with hundreds of conflicts over the future of a community, group, location or business that were "resolved" when one of the parties stepped ahead and destroyed what was there. With the original point of contention destroyed, the debates would fall to the wayside. Archive Team believes that by duplicated condemned data, the conversation and debate can continue, as well as the richness and insight gained by keeping the materials. Our projects have ranged in size from a single volunteer downloading the data to a small-but-critical site, to over 100 volunteers stepping forward to acquire terabytes of user-created data to save for future generations.

The main site for Archive Team is at archiveteam.org and contains up to the date information on various projects, manifestos, plans and walkthroughs.

This collection contains the output of many Archive Team projects, both ongoing and completed. Thanks to the generous providing of disk space by the Internet Archive, multi-terabyte datasets can be made available, as well as in use by the Wayback Machine, providing a path back to lost websites and work.

Our collection has grown to the point of having sub-collections for the type of data we acquire. If you are seeking to browse the contents of these collections, the Wayback Machine is the best first stop. Otherwise, you are free to dig into the stacks to see what you may find.

The Archive Team Panic Downloads are full pulldowns of currently extant websites, meant to serve as emergency backups for needed sites that are in danger of closing, or which will be missed dearly if suddenly lost due to hard drive crashes or server failures.

Notice Regarding the Removal of BUSD and Conversion of BUSD to FDUSD

2023-11-29 08:13

This is a general announcement. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

This is to notify you that as Paxos has halted minting of new BUSD, Binance will cease support for BUSD products on 2023-12-15. Please be assured that BUSD will always be backed 1:1 by USD. Paxos has stated that BUSD will remain supported by Paxos and redeemable through at least February 2024.

Users are encouraged to withdraw or convert their BUSD assets into other available assets on Binance before 2023-12-15. Users may trade their BUSD balances for FDUSD at zero trading fees, or convert their BUSD balances to FDUSD on Binance Convert at a 1:1 conversion rate with zero fees.

From 2024-01-02 03:00 (UTC), BUSD withdrawals will be disabled, and any remaining BUSD balances in users’ Binance accounts will be automatically converted to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate. Thereafter, users may continue depositing BUSD into Binance and can manually convert their BUSD to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate until further notice. 

In addition, collateral assets of Binance-Peg BUSD will also be converted to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate, anticipated to take place around December 2023. A separate announcement will be posted to notify users when the collateral conversion is fully completed. 

The following changes for products involving BUSD will be effected on the dates listed below:

Spot:

  • Binance will remove and cease trading on the following spot trading pairs:

    • From 2023-12-11 03:00 (UTC): AGIX/BUSD, BUSD/BIDR, BUSD/BRL, BUSD/DAI, BUSD/TRY, EUR/BUSD, FET/BUSD

    • From 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC): GALA/BUSD, INJ/BUSD, LINK/BUSD, LUNA/BUSD, LUNC/BUSD, RUNE/BUSD, SHIB/BUSD, TUSD/BUSD, WBTC/BUSD

    • From 2023-12-15 03:00 (UTC): ADA/BUSD, ALGO/BUSD, ATOM/BUSD, AVAX/BUSD, BCH/BUSD, BNB/BUSD, DOGE/BUSD 

    • From 2023-12-15 05:00 (UTC): BTC/BUSD, BUSD/USDT, DOT/BUSD, ETH/BUSD, FDUSD/BUSD, FTM/BUSD, LTC/BUSD, MATIC/BUSD, NEAR/BUSD, SOL/BUSD, XRP/BUSD 

  • All open spot orders with respect to the above trading pairs will automatically be closed when trading ceases in the relevant trading pair.

  • Binance will terminate Spot Trading Bots services for the aforementioned spot trading pairs respectively at 2023-12-11 03:00 (UTC), 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC), 2023-12-15 03:00 (UTC) and 2023-12-15 05:00 (UTC), where applicable. Users are strongly advised to update and/or cancel their Spot Trading Bots prior to the cessation of Spot Trading Bots services to avoid any potential losses.

Futures & Margin:

BUSD will be removed from Futures and Margin as per the schedule announced here.

Deposits, Withdrawals & Conversions:

  • From 2024-01-02 03:00 (UTC):

    • Withdrawals of BUSD (ERC-20) tokens via Ethereum network will cease, and BUSD withdrawals will no longer be supported. Withdrawals of Binance-Peg BUSD tokens via BNB Chain, Avalanche, Polygon, Tron and Optimism networks ceased at 2023-09-07 00:00 (UTC). 

    • Deposits of BUSD and Binance-Peg BUSD via the below networks will be temporarily suspended. Please ensure that you leave sufficient time for your BUSD deposits to be fully processed prior to this time.

      • BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)

      • BNB Beacon Chain (BEP2)

      • Ethereum (ERC20)

      • Tron (TRC20)

      • Polygon

      • Optimism

      • AVAX C-Chain

    • All BUSD balances in Spot and Funding Wallets will be automatically converted to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate. The conversion may take approximately 24 hours or longer. Nevertheless, all users are encouraged to withdraw or convert their BUSD assets into other available assets on Binance before 2023-12-15.

  • After the conversion, BUSD deposits on the aforementioned networks will be resumed until further notice. A separate announcement will be posted to notify users when BUSD deposits will be resumed. 

  • After 2024-01-02 03:00 (UTC), any unprocessed or subsequent deposits of BUSD will be credited as BUSD. Users can convert BUSD into FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate via Binance Convert with zero fees until further notice.

Convert & OTC:

  • Moving forward, users can continue converting their BUSD balances to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate using Binance Convert and Binance OTC until further notice.

  • At 2023-12-15 03:00 (UTC), Convert will delist all BUSD pairs whilst retaining the feature of converting BUSD to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate. 

  • Please note that the conversion of BUSD to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate will be temporarily suspended from 2024-01-02 02:00 (UTC) till completion of the aforementioned balance conversion event. A separate announcement will be posted to notify users when the conversion of BUSD to FDUSD at a 1:1 conversion rate resumes. 

  • Please be aware that the 1:1 conversion rate is only applicable for converting BUSD to FDUSD, and not the other way around.

Loans:

  • BUSD has been delisted as a collateral asset and loanable asset. At 2023-10-25 07:59 (UTC), Binance Loans (Flexible and Stable Rates) liquidated outstanding loan orders with BUSD as borrowed asset and collateral asset.

  • From 2023-12-13 04:00 (UTC), BUSD will be delisted as a collateral asset from VIP Loan.

Earn:

  • From 2023-12-11 00:00 (UTC), Dual Investment will convert all BUSD open positions to FDUSD in order to protect users and ensure that all open BUSD positions will settle at a 1:1 conversion rate in the more liquid FDUSD. Leading up to 2023-12-11, Dual Investment will gradually stop offering all BUSD products with settlement dates after 2023-12-08. This arrangement will prevail over all previous announcements.

  • From 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC), Binance Simple Earn will cease support for BUSD. Simple Earn Flexible Products for BUSD will no longer be available for subscription and existing BUSD balances in Simple Earn will be automatically redeemed and credited to users’ Spot Wallets. Users are strongly encouraged to redeem their assets from BUSD Flexible Products beforehand.

Card:

  • From 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC), BUSD will be delisted as a spend currency for Binance Card.

Pay

  • From 2023-11-30 03:00 (UTC), Binance Pay will remove BUSD from the list of supported cryptocurrencies on Binance Pay Red Packet Creation.

  • From 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC), Binance Pay will remove BUSD from the list of supported cryptocurrencies on other features of Binance Pay.

Gift Card

  • From 2023-12-13 03:00 (UTC): 

  • Binance Gift Card will stop supporting BUSD redemption.

  • Any unredeemed BUSD Gift Cards will be redeemable for FDUSD on a 1:1 conversion rate.  

We thank you for your support as we continue to build the crypto ecosystem in a way that promotes transparency and long-term, sustainable growth. Should you have any queries, please reach out to our Customer Support Team for assistance.

Note: Where any discrepancy arises between the translated versions and the original English version, the English version shall prevail.

Thanks for your support!

Binance Team

2023-11-29

Note: This announcement was updated on 2023-12-06 to reflect changes to the timeline for BUSD deposits, withdrawals and conversions, and to clarify that all users will be eligible for the automatic conversion of BUSD balances to FDUSD. It was previously updated on 2023-11-29 to adjust the delisting time for the FDUSD/BUSD spot trading pair from 2023-12-11 03:00 (UTC) to 2023-12-15 05:00 (UTC).
