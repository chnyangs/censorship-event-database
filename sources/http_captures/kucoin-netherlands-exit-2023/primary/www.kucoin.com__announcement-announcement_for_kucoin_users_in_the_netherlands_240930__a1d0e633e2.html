Announcement for KuCoin Users in the Netherlands

09/30/2024, 08:00:00

Dear KuCoin Users,

In order to comply with the requirements of Dutch laws and regulations, as well as instructions from the De Nederlandsche Bank, we would like to inform you about some important changes that will affect our users in the Netherlands.

For New Users: Starting from September 30, 2024 (UTC+0), new users from the Netherlands will no longer be able to register on KuCoin.

For Existing Users: Existing users from the Netherlands who have not completed their KYC (Know Your Customer) verification process will no longer be able to do so starting from September 30, 2024 (UTC+0).

For all other users, all account functionalities, including deposits, withdrawals and trading will remain unaffected. Rest assured that your account remains safe with us and the security of your account assets is our utmost priority. We are committed to always ensuring that your assets remain safe and secure.

We understand that these changes may cause inconvenience, and we regret any disruption this may cause. We appreciate your understanding and continued support.

If you have any questions, please visit our Help Center for more information, join our Telegram Group, or contact our Online Support Team. We are here to provide you with more assistance and guidance.

The KuCoin Team
