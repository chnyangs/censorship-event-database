They are enumerated here as **three separate S5_corporate candidates** because each protocol's frontend integration of TRM Labs / Chainalysis APIs was an independent corporate-policy decision; if the schema treats them as subsumed by the OFAC trigger event, fold rather than admit.
- **Apple App Store removal (2024-01-10)** and **Google Play removal (2024-01-14)** in India are downstream of the `india-fiu-offshore-vda-block-2023` trigger already in corpus. Enumerated here as separate L4 candidates because the app-store removals are corporate-policy actions by Apple / Google distinct from the FIU notice itself.
- **MetaMask / Infura 2022-03-03 Venezuela block** was an over-broad enforcement of 2022-02 Donetsk/Luhansk sanctions and was reversed within days. Retained as P0 because it is the cleanest L3 IP-geo enforcement event in the corpus (orthogonal mechanism to address-set screening).
- **Trust Wallet, Rabby, Frame, WalletConnect** did not surface concrete dated policy-change events beyond ToS boilerplate; not enumerated.
- **Lido / Compound / Curve / SushiSwap governance/frontend events**: the literature references several governance debates but the specific dated decisions are either pre-existing pause/risk actions (Compound Proposal 131 is a market-risk pause, not a censorship event) or insufficiently anchored to a single trigger date. Not enumerated.
- **Phantom / Coinbase Wallet scam-token blocklists** are user-protection actions, not government-driven censorship; not enumerated.
